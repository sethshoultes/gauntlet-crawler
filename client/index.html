On a phone or tablet, an oversized touch d-pad and fire/auto-fire/potion buttons appear automatically. Standard USB/Bluetooth gamepads (Xbox, PlayStation, generic) work too — up to 3 extra controllers open extra local heroes in the same room.

Your health ticks down every second. Eat food. Shoot generators before they flood the room. Keys open doors. Potions clear the screen. Find the exit. It never ends.
