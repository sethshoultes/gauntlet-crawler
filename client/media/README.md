# Title media (AI-generated)

These assets back the attract-mode title screen and the `/trailer.html` page (GitHub issue #21).

- `title-backdrop.webp` / `title-loop.mp4` — the dungeon-hall backdrop and its animated loop
  (drifting ghosts, flickering torches), used behind the in-engine attract-mode canvas and as
  its `prefers-reduced-motion`/autoplay-failure fallback poster.
- `title-card.webp` — the same scene with the "GAUNTLET CRAWLER" logo baked in, used for the
  Open Graph / Twitter social preview image and as the trailer's poster frame.
- `trailer.mp4` — a short animated trailer (heroes marching into the dungeon door), played with
  controls on `/trailer.html`.

All four were AI-generated on 2026-09-05: still images with **Nano Banana Pro**, video with
**Seedance 2.5** (via Higgsfield). They are not hand-authored art and may be regenerated or
replaced at any time — treat them as placeholder production assets, not a fixed asset contract.
